# Contributing to Chasidica

Chasidica is in design and mechanical-prototype development. Start with [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and the [production gates](docs/production.md). Feedback on a repeatable decision is more useful at this stage than a large unrelated feature.

- Keep changes focused and explain the gameplay or technical reason.
- Preserve unrelated work and maintain the distinction between a concept, a working implementation, and evidence from playtesting.
- For rules changes, run `node design/check-fixtures.mjs` and explain any intentionally changed results.
- Use fictional people and institutions. Check cultural details with informed reviewers; do not turn assumptions into claims about real communities.
- Do not publish private playtest feedback or recordings without consent.

By intentionally submitting a contribution, you agree to license it under the applicable terms in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md): GPL-3.0-or-later for software and CC BY-SA 4.0 for original prose and standalone creative assets, unless separately agreed and recorded. You retain your rights; there is no copyright assignment or automatic proprietary-relicensing grant.

Only submit material you have the authority to share. Identify third-party components, keep their notices, and document their sources and licenses. Disclose AI-assisted/generated assets and record the tool, prompt or relevant provenance, and your review. Do not claim that an AI-generated concept is a screenshot or a human artist's original work.
